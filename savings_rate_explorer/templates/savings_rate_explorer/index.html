Why income doesn't matter here: if you save X% of your income, your expenses are automatically the other (100−X)%, and your monthly contribution is X% too — both scale with income equally, so income cancels out of the years-to-FI calculation entirely. A person earning 500,000/year saving 24% reaches FI in exactly the same number of years as someone earning 50,000/year saving 24%. Only the rate matters.
